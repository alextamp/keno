Child Safety Standards

Keno — hyper-local events for Greek university students · Last updated 19 August 2026

Zero tolerance

Keno has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This applies to every part of the app — profiles, events, messages, group chats, and photos. Any account found creating, distributing, or soliciting content that sexually exploits or endangers a minor is permanently banned immediately upon discovery, without warning, regardless of any other policy on second chances.

Who Keno is for

Keno is built for current university students in Greece. Sign-up requires a valid university email address (ending in .gr); accounts without one go through a manual ID-photo review by our admin team before they can use the app. This is meant to keep the app to its intended audience of university students, not a general-audience social network.

How we prevent and detect abuse

  • Identity verification: every account is verified either through a university email or a reviewed ID photo before it can fully use the app.
  • Reporting on everything: every profile, event, and message can be reported directly from within the app, by any user, at any time.
  • Automated image moderation: every photo uploaded to the app (profile photos, event photos, chat images, the event photo wall) is automatically scanned before it's shown to anyone, and removed immediately if it's flagged as adult, violent, or otherwise unsafe content.
  • Automated text moderation: free-text fields (bios, event descriptions, chat messages) are filtered for known-abusive content before they're posted.
  • Blocking: any user can block any other user, immediately cutting off all contact between them.
  • Human review: our team reviews every report. Reports are addressed within 24 hours; a second confirmed violation results in account suspension.

Reporting a concern

If you see something that concerns you — inside the app or otherwise — report it directly from the profile, event, or message in question, or email keno.app.official@gmail.com directly. We review every report we receive.

Where we're legally required to, we report violations involving the exploitation or endangerment of a minor to the appropriate law enforcement authorities.
